Questions? We Have Answers.

What is a B1/B2 visa?

B1/B2 is the USA visitor visa. B1 is for short business trips, such as meetings or conferences. B2 is for tourism, visiting family or friends, or medical visits. Most people apply for one combined B1/B2 visa. Only the US government decides who receives a visa.

What does Aarviko do?

We are a private appointment-assistance service in Hyderabad. We watch for open USA B1/B2 visa appointment slots and help you book OFC biometrics and consular interview slots through the official system, when availability permits. We are not the US government and we do not issue visas.

What can you not promise?

We cannot promise any slot date, any earlier date or any visa result. Slot availability is set by the official system, and visa decisions are made only by US officials. What we do promise is clear steps, honest updates and personal support.

How does slot monitoring work?

We check for open slots every day, all day, 365 days a year. When a suitable slot appears, we contact you and, with your go-ahead, help you book it through the official system. Slots can fill quickly, so fast replies from you help.

What information do you need from me?

Usually your name as on your passport, your phone number, the city you prefer for your appointments and your preferred date range. The official booking may need a few more details. Our team will tell you exactly what is needed on your free consultation call.

Is the visa fee included in your price?

No. The US government visa fee is paid separately, directly to the government. We do not control it, and we do not quote it. Our charges cover appointment assistance only. We explain our pricing clearly before you decide to go ahead.

Can you get me an earlier date?

We cannot promise it. If an earlier slot opens, we can help you try to move to it, as far as the official system and its rules allow. Some changes may be limited, so please talk to us before you cancel or change anything yourself.

What is the difference between an OFC appointment and an interview appointment?

The OFC (biometrics) appointment is for biometrics, usually fingerprints and a photo. The consular interview appointment is your conversation with a US officer at the consulate. They are two separate bookings, and we can help with both when slots are open.

How fast will you call me back?

After you send a message or WhatsApp us, our team will call you back within 2 hours, Monday to Saturday. Messages sent outside those days are answered when the team is next in. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +91 95022 90703.

Is my data safe?

We ask only for details we need and handle them with care. Never share passwords or OTPs in public places, on social media or in group chats. Share sensitive details only on a private call or chat with our team. If anything feels unsafe, tell us right away.
