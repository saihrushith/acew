Frequently asked questions

Questions About Visa Services

Which visa categories can I enquire about?

You can enquire about tourist, visitor, business, student, work and family visa assistance. Availability depends on the destination and your requirements.

Does AARVIKO provide USA visa assistance in Hyderabad?

You can enquire about USA visitor visa, B1/B2 visa and student visa application guidance.

What documents are required for a visa application?

Required documents depend on the destination, visa category and applicant's circumstances. Our team can help you understand relevant documentation requirements.

Can AARVIKO guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa approval is decided by the relevant government or immigration authority. AARVIKO can provide application guidance and support, but cannot guarantee approval.

Does AARVIKO provide both Recruitment and Visa Services?

Yes. Recruitment supports businesses and job seekers, while Visa Services supports individuals planning international travel, study or work.

How do I contact AARVIKO?

You can contact us through the website enquiry form, phone or WhatsApp to discuss Recruitment or Visa Services.
